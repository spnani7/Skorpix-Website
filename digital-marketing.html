FAQ

Questions about digital marketing

How is pricing decided?

We discuss your goal and budget, then send a written quote. Ad spend is separate from our fee.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay the ad budget directly to Google or Meta. Our fee covers planning, setup and management.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Results depend on your market, budget and offer. We share what we plan to do and review progress with you.

Can I start with just one service?

Yes. You can start with Google Ads, Meta Ads, Instagram management or content creation on its own.

Do you also fix my Google Business Profile?

Yes. A complete Google profile makes ads and content work harder, and we can handle both.
