FAQ

Questions about 360° tours

What is a Google 360° virtual tour?

An interactive walkthrough of your business, published on Google Maps and Search so customers can look around before they visit.

How long does a shoot take?

Usually 1 to 3 hours depending on the size of the space.

How long until it is live?

Editing and publishing follow the shoot. Google can take several business days to show the tour.

Can I choose what the tour shows?

Yes. Private areas can be left out, and faces are blurred as Google requires.

Can I use the tour on my website?

Yes. We give you an embed code and a shareable link.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. We are based in Chennai and travel for 360° shoots across India.
