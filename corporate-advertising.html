How it works

From brief to campaign

A clear process helps your team review the options before committing budget.

  1. Share your brief

    Tell us your audience, cities, campaign dates and budget range.

  2. Review options

    We check suitable campuses, formats, availability and indicative costs.

  3. Confirm the plan

    Review the proposal and confirm scope, approvals, schedule and terms.

  4. Launch and report

    After approvals, payment and creative delivery, dRSTi coordinates execution and agreed reporting.

Our role. Skorpix is a channel partner of dRSTi Communications. We help brands and agencies explore workplace media options in corporate campuses and tech parks. Inventory, proposals and campaign execution are provided by dRSTi Communications, and all proposals are subject to inventory confirmation, campus permissions and the media partner’s terms. Placements and rates are confirmed for each campaign.
FAQ

Questions about campus advertising

Which campuses can I advertise in?

Locations depend on current inventory and partner approvals. Tell us your target cities and audience, and we share suitable options.

How are rates decided?

Rates depend on format, location, duration and availability. We share indicative costs first and confirm final rates for your campaign.

Who runs the campaign?

dRSTi Communications and its campus partners deliver the media and execution. Skorpix introduces and coordinates your enquiry.

Can I choose more than one format?

Yes. Many campaigns combine screens, static media and on-ground activations.

Are the campaigns shown above Skorpix work?

No. They were delivered by dRSTi Communications and are shown to illustrate the formats and placements available.
