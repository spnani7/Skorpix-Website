FAQ

Questions about Google Business Profile

My call button or phone number is missing. Can you fix it?

Yes. We file the appeal with Google and follow up until it is resolved. Timing depends on Google, usually a few days to a few weeks. You pay after the number is live.

My listing is unverified or suspended. Can you help?

Yes. We handle the documents, video or postcard verification and appeals. Timelines depend on Google, so we cannot promise a fixed date.

Do you guarantee a number one ranking?

No one honestly can. We improve your profile, photos and activity so you have the best chance of showing up near the top.

How long does profile setup take?

Most profiles are set up within a few days once we have your business details. Verification timing depends on Google.

Do you work outside Chennai?

Yes. Profile services are done remotely, so we work with businesses across India.

Can you manage several locations?

Yes. We keep names, categories and details consistent across every outlet and clean up duplicates.
